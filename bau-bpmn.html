BAU Content Workflow

Monthly-planned video content · to-be process (BPMN 2.0) · Draft v2 · 12 Nov 2026

Every hand-off and approval moves into the platform. AI turns the script into mood board visuals; video production stays in the creative team's own tools.

Phase A ends with a link event that Phase B picks up.

Phase A · Brief to approved mood boardTrend Library for brainstorming, AI-generated visuals, then internal and client review

Phase B · Production to final approvalTwo review gates on the finished video: internal, then client

Notation

Happens inside the platform
Happens in an external tool (named in grey)

Decision (approved or not)

Loop: repeats until the brief is clear

Confirmed rules

  • After any client revision, the revised board or content goes back through internal review before it returns to the client.
  • The copywriter and art director revise the script and board together in one step.
  • The client's approval or feedback in the platform alerts both Account and Creative.
  • The script and mood board are written, assembled, reviewed and approved inside the platform. Google Slides is no longer used for the board.

Still open

  • Is there a revision loop after the internal review of the finished video? Assumed yes in Phase B.
  • Is Suno used for music in production?
  • Waiting time between steps, especially review turnaround, to complete the baseline.
