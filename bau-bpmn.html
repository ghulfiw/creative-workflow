Rules in this version

  • Account prepares and finalizes the brief; the SLA clock starts there. The Strategic Planner then completes it with a trend direction from the Trend Library (2 h).
  • No brainstorm. The copywriter fills a short form to prompt the AI for script options, picks the best one and edits it by hand or by prompt. AI then adds still images, which the art director refines by prompt. This takes 2 h in total.
  • Revisions after internal or client feedback are made by prompt by the copywriter and art director together.
  • After the client's content revision, only an internal re-review follows; the process is then complete. If the client still wants changes, a new brief starts the process again.
  • After a client board revision, internal and client re-review the board together in one session.
  • SLA: 5 business days from brief final.
