Assumptions

  • BAU uses the final BAU timeline: one brief, 5 video variants, 30 h.
  • Trendjacking uses the same steps and working times as final BAU, except that Account's brief prep is not a separate step: Account and the Strategic Planner complete the brief together (4 h of working time), inside the 2-day clock.
  • Trendjacking covers 3 video variants instead of 5: video production and content revision working times are scaled to 3/5 of BAU (3 h → 1 h 48 min, 2 h → 1 h 12 min). Reviews keep the same working time, as in BAU.
  • Fast-track turnaround on Trendjacking: internal reviews picked up within 20 min, revisions start as soon as feedback lands, and client waits are halved (board review 1 h, content review and re-reviews 30 min). Brainstorm pickup (1 h) and video generation and rendering (1 h) are unchanged.
Sources: BAU SLA (final timeline) · Trendjacking SLA draft · answers from Ghulfi, 13 Nov 2026.
