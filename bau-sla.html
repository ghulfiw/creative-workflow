What each side commits to

Agency

  • Content complete within 5 business days of brief final, including one revision at each gate.
  • Account prepares and finalizes the brief within 1 working hour; the Strategic Planner completes it with a trend direction within 2 working hours.
  • Script and still images ready within 2 working hours of the completed brief: AI script options, picked and edited by the copywriter, then AI still images refined by the art director.
  • Internal board review done within 1 h 30 min (40 min to pick up, 50 min to review); internal content review within 2 hours (1 h to pick up, 1 h to review); internal content re-review within 1 h 10 min.
  • Revisions returned within 1.5 hours (board) or 3 hours (content: 1 h to start, 2 h to revise).
  • After the content revision and internal re-review, the agency sends the final content and closes the task.

Client

  • Brief final at least 1 week before the date content must be ready to post.
  • Board review within 2 working hours (1 h to open, 1 h to review). Content review within 2 working hours (1 h to open, 1 h to review).
  • Board re-review together with Cheil's internal reviewers, in one session within 2 working hours.
  • One combined set of feedback per review, in the platform. Changes requested after the final content start a new brief.
  • Any delay on the client side moves the deadline by the same number of hours.

Assumptions

  • 1 business day = 8 working hours. The SLA is 40 working hours from brief final to completion.
  • Working times cover all 5 videos together. The Strategic Planner's brief completion (2 h) and the AI script and still images step (2 h) are working time with no waiting.
  • Waiting times: 40 minutes to 1 hour for internal reviewers to pick up a review, 1 hour of video generation and rendering, and 1 hour for the client to open a review.
  • One revision at each gate. After a board revision, internal and client re-review the board together. After a content revision, only an internal re-review follows, and the process is complete.
  • Each review covers all 5 videos at once.
