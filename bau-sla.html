Draft SLA: brief final to final approval

One 15-second video · BAU workflow · one revision at each gate · 1 business day = 8 working hours · Draft v0.2 · 12 Nov 2026

One video fits in 5 business days with more than a day to spare, if the client reviews within 2–3 working hours. More videos only add production and revision time, so a brief fits 5 days as long as each producer has no more than 3 videos.

What each side commits to

Agency

  • Final approval within 5 business days of brief final, including one revision at each gate.
  • Account prepares the brief and hands it to the creative team within 1 working hour of brief final.
  • Brainstorm session starts within 1 working hour of the hand-off; script ready 3 hours later.
  • Internal board review done within 1 h 40 min (40 min to pick up, 1 h to review); internal content review within 2 hours (1 h to pick up, 1 h to review). Internal board re-review within 1 h 40 min (40 min to pick up, 1 h to review); internal content re-review within 1 h 10 min (40 min to pick up, 30 min to review).
  • Revisions returned within 1.5 hours (board) or 3 hours (content: 1 h to start, 2 h to revise).

Client

  • Brief final at least 1 week before the date content must be approved and ready to post.
  • Board review within 3 working hours (2 h to open, 1 h to review). Content review within 2 working hours (1 h to open, 1 h to review). Re-reviews within 2 working hours.
  • One combined set of feedback per review, in the platform.
  • Any delay on the client side moves the deadline by the same number of hours. A second revision round at a gate adds its own time.

Scaling to more videos

Each extra video adds production and content revision time for whoever produces it. Everything else stays the same, including review time for up to 20 short videos, since reviewers only watch and judge them. Move the sliders to simulate a brief.

Assumptions in this draft

  • 1 business day = 8 working hours. The SLA is 40 working hours from brief final to final approval.
  • Working times are the workshop estimates for one 15-second video. The AI mood board starts as soon as the script is submitted: 20 minutes of generation, then 40 minutes for the AD to revise it by prompt. Video production is 3 hours of prompting and editing (Google Flow, After Effects, possibly Suno).
  • Waiting times are estimates: 1 hour to get the brainstorm session together, 40 minutes to 1 hour for internal reviewers to pick up a review, no wait for the AI mood board, 1 hour of video generation and rendering, and 2 hours (board), 1 hour (content), 1 hour (board re-review) or 1.5 hours (content re-review) for the client to open a review.
  • Internal reviewers: ECD and AD/AAD for the board; ECD, CGH, AD/AAD and AM for content. Reviewers in a step review at the same time.
  • Review working time stays the same for up to 20 short videos per review.
  • Exactly one revision at each gate. Every revision goes back through internal review before the client sees it again.
  • The agency does no work after final client approval.
