What each side commits to on the fast track

Agency

  • Strategic Planner completes the brief (trend insight, angle, references) within 4 working hours of brief final.
  • Brainstorm (Copywriter + AD on call) starts within 1 working hour of the completed brief.
  • Internal reviews and re-reviews picked up within 20 minutes of the urgent alert.
  • Revisions start as soon as feedback arrives: board 1 h, content 1 h.
  • The platform records the SLA result. Late content is still posted.

Client

  • First reviews (board, content) within 2 working hours. Re-reviews within 1 working hour.
  • A named approver is available during the 2-day window.
  • One combined set of feedback per review, in the platform.
  • Any delay on the client side moves the deadline by the same number of hours.

Assumptions in this draft

  • 2 business days = 16 working hours, from brief final (by Account) to final client approval.
  • The Strategic Planner's brief completion (4 h, counted as working time with no waiting) is inside the clock.
  • Same steps, gates, reviewers and working times as the BAU SLA draft, one revision at each gate.
  • Working time is not compressed. Only waiting is.
  • Video generation and rendering keep their 2 hours of waiting.
  • Live board review: 1 h to get everyone in a session, 30 min review, 1 h revision, then 30 min for the client to sign off in the platform.
