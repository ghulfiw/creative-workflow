What each side commits to

Agency

  • Content complete within 3 business days of brief final.
  • Account prepares and finalizes the brief within 1 working hour; the Strategic Planner completes it with a trend direction within 2 working hours.
  • Script and still images ready within 2 working hours of the completed brief: AI script options, picked and edited by the copywriter, then AI still images refined by the art director.
  • Internal reviews picked up within 20–30 minutes of the alert.
  • Revisions returned within 1.5 hours (board) or 2 hours (content).
  • After the content revision and internal re-review, the agency sends the final content and closes the task. Late content is still posted, and the platform records the miss.

Client

  • Board feedback within 2 working hours (1 h to open, 1 h to review), one round only.
  • Content review within 1 h 30 min (30 min to open, 1 h to review).
  • A named approver available during the 3 days, and one combined set of feedback per review, in the platform.
  • Changes requested after the final content start a new brief. Any delay on the client side moves the deadline by the same number of hours.

Assumptions

  • 1 business day = 8 working hours. The SLA is 24 working hours from brief final to completion.
  • Working times cover all 3 videos together. The Strategic Planner's brief completion (2 h) and the AI script and still images step (2 h) are working time with no waiting.
  • Fast-track waits: internal reviews picked up within 20–30 minutes, 30 minutes of video generation and rendering, and the client opens the board review within 1 hour and the content review within 30 minutes.
  • One round of client board feedback, then straight to production. After the content revision, only an internal re-review follows, and the process is complete.
