Trendjacking Content Workflow

Occasion- or trend-driven video content · to-be process (BPMN 2.0) · Draft v2 · 13 Nov 2026

Same gates, reviewers and 15–30-second format as BAU, on a 2-business-day clock. The client starts it, or the Strategic Planner spots an opportunity in the Trend Library and proposes it. The Planner also completes the brief before creative work starts. Steps marked NEW differ from BAU.

Confirmed rules

  • The client starts every trendjacking request. Requests cover both planned occasions (for example a cup final) and trends that appear without warning.
  • The Strategist AI keeps the Trend Library up to date from social listening. The Strategic Planner analyses the library, identifies opportunities and writes proposals for the client, who decides whether to go ahead.
  • Trendjacking briefs are usually less polished than BAU briefs. After Account finalises the brief, the Strategic Planner completes it (trend insight, angle, references) before it goes to the creative team.
  • Same as BAU: 15–30-second videos, a client board approval, the same internal reviewers, and internal review after every client revision.
  • SLA: 2 business days from brief final to final client approval.
  • If the deadline is missed, the content is still posted. The platform records the SLA miss.

Still open

  • Brand-safety and rights check (music, memes, references): nobody owns it yet.
  • Who posts the content: the client or the agency.
  • Whether the 2-day clock counts from the request or from brief final (drawn here from brief final, as in BAU), and whether the Planner's brief completion counts inside it.
  • Whether the Planner presents opportunities to the client directly, or through Account (drawn here through Account).
